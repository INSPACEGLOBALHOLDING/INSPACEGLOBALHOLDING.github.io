01 — About the Group

A Singapore base for long-cycle regional infrastructure.

Inspace Global Holding Pte. Ltd. was incorporated in the Republic of Singapore as the parent entity for industrial and energy infrastructure projects across Indonesia and Southeast Asia. The Group originates, structures, and oversees capital-intensive assets through dedicated local operating subsidiaries.

Purpose

Sustainable transformation

Heavy industry underpins every developing economy yet is among the slowest to decarbonize. The Group exists to close that gap with technically rigorous, capital-disciplined integrated complexes.

Approach

Clean tech · logistics · partnership

Integrated sites that combine production, energy generation, port logistics, and digital infrastructure on a single permitted footprint — engineered from day one for international standards.

Mandate

Industrial & energy EPC platforms

Green steel complexes, hybrid power facilities, port-integrated logistics — structured for EPC engagement, local partnership, and international institutional comfort.

02 — Current Operations

Flagship development in Aceh, Indonesia.

The Group's current operational focus is the Aceh Integrated Green Steel Complex (AGSC) — a multi-asset metallurgical and clean-energy site within the KEK Southwest Aceh Special Economic Zone.

Project · Flagship

Aceh Integrated Green Steel Complex

KEK Southwest Aceh · Sumatra · Indonesia

Active · EPC Engagement
Configuration

Hydrogen-ready DRI-EAF green-steel line, thin-slab casting and rolling, co-located AI supercomputing center using steel waste-heat for cooling. Phase-1 bridge asset and integrated port logistics through Surin Port.

03 — Group Structure

Singapore parent, regional operating subsidiaries.

A two-tier corporate architecture: an offshore Singapore holding entity for international capital engagement, and onshore operating SPVs aligned with each jurisdiction's regulatory and permitting framework.

Parent

Inspace Global Holding Pte. Ltd.

Incorporated in the Republic of Singapore

Group-level capital structuring, EPC partner engagement, and international counterparty interface. Holds equity participation in regional operating entities and coordinates cross-border financing.

Operating Subsidiary (Indonesia)

On-the-ground operating entity for the Aceh integrated industrial complex. Coordinates permitting, KEK Special Economic Zone administration, local procurement, and Indonesian regulatory interface.
