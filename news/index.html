03Three production pathways: integrated BF-BOF, scrap-based EAF, and hydrogen direct reduction三大生产路径：一体化高炉转炉、废钢电弧炉与氢基直接还原›
Modern steelmaking runs on two established routes plus one breakthrough low-emission pathway. Integrated mills (BF-BOF) convert iron ore and coking coal through blast furnaces and basic oxygen furnaces — high emissions, with CO2 and slag as byproducts. Mini-mills (EAF) melt recycled ferrous scrap with high-voltage electricity for a low-to-moderate emissions profile. Hydrogen direct reduction (H2-DRI) processes high-grade iron ore pellets in hydrogen shaft furnaces paired with EAFs, powered by green hydrogen and electricity — an ultra-low emissions route whose primary byproduct is water vapor rather than carbon dioxide.现代钢铁生产依托两条成熟路径与一条突破性低排放路径。一体化钢厂（BF-BOF）以铁矿石和焦煤为原料，通过高炉与碱性氧气转炉冶炼——排放高，副产品为二氧化碳与炉渣。小型钢厂（EAF）以高压电力熔炼回收废钢，排放属于中低水平。氢基直接还原（H2-DRI）则以高品位铁矿球团为原料，在氢气竖炉搭配电弧炉中冶炼，能源来自绿氢与电力——属于超低排放路径，主要副产品是水蒸气而非二氧化碳。
04Secondary metallurgy and digital automation: the new technical differentiator for high-grade steel二次冶金与数字化自动化：高等级钢材的新技术分水岭›
Because EAFs rely heavily on recycled scrap with variable trace impurities, secondary metallurgy has become the key differentiator for high-grade steel. Vacuum degassing (VD) places molten steel in a sealed vacuum chamber, forcing trapped hydrogen and nitrogen to bubble out — hydrogen removal is mandatory to prevent hydrogen embrittlement in pipeline, defense, and automotive steels. Twin ladle furnace (LF) stations with alternating power supply — such as the 180-ton unit Primetals recently supplied to Outokumpu Stainless in Alabama — eliminate dead time between heats by alloying one ladle while the next is prepared. Computer vision and robotic systems, like Hyundai Steel's newly online TrimRob, now handle dangerous finishing work, sample gathering, and real-time surface defect detection.由于电弧炉高度依赖含有不稳定微量杂质的回收废钢，二次冶金已成为生产高等级钢材的关键技术分水岭。真空脱气（VD）将钢水置于密封真空室中，迫使溶解的氢气与氮气逸出——脱氢是防止管线钢、国防用钢与汽车用钢发生氢脆（内部微裂纹）的必要工序。采用交替供电的双工位钢包精炼炉（LF）——例如普锐特冶金技术（Primetals）近期向美国阿拉巴马州奥托昆普不锈钢厂交付的 180 吨机组——在一个钢包进行精确合金化的同时准备下一个钢包，消除了炉次之间的空档时间。计算机视觉与机器人系统（如现代制铁近期上线的 TrimRob 系统）则承担危险的精整作业、取样与实时表面缺陷检测，显著降低了废品率。
