Global steel: upheaval, decarbonization and digital transformation全球钢铁制造业：市场剧变、脱碳工程与数字化转型

Global steelmaking has entered a decisive era. High energy costs, hardening trade barriers and looming sustainability mandates are testing incumbents, even as a wave of green and digital breakthroughs accelerates toward commercial market.全球钢铁制造业正迎来一个决定性的时代。高昂的能源成本、日益严峻的贸易壁垒，以及迫在眉睫的可持续发展指令，正深刻考验着传统钢铁企业，而一系列数字化与绿色工程突破也正加速推向商业市场。

01EU tightens import quotas and carbon-tariff walls欧盟收紧进口配额与碳关税壁垒›
International steel trade is contracting sharply. From 1 July 2026 the EU replaces its long-running safeguard measures, expiring at end-June, with a new tariff-rate-quota (TRQ) regime: duty-free import quotas are cut by roughly 47% versus 2024, the over-quota tariff jumps from 25% to 50%, and a new “melt and pour” origin declaration is added to curb transshipment relabeling. This heavily protectionist mechanism aims to insulate environmentally conscious domestic mills from cheap, high-carbon foreign steel. In parallel, the EU’s Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) entered its definitive charging phase on 1 January 2026, requiring importers to buy CBAM certificates for embedded emissions or face steep costs.国际钢铁贸易格局正急剧收紧。2026 年 7 月 1 日，欧盟以全新的关税配额（TRQ）制度取代于 6 月底到期的保障措施：免税进口配额较 2024 年削减约 47%，配额用尽后的超额关税由 25% 一举提高至 50%，并新增“熔炼与浇铸地（melt and pour）”原产地申报要求，以遏制转口换标。这一重保护机制旨在将注重环保的本土钢企，与海外廉价、高碳排钢材隔离开来。与此并行，欧盟碳边境调节机制（CBAM）已于 2026 年 1 月 1 日进入正式征费阶段，进口商须按产品隐含的碳排放购买 CBAM 证书，否则将承担高额成本。
02US tariff incentives for domestic steel美国对本土钢材的关税激励政策›
In the US, steel manufacturing is riding a large capex wave. Under the Section 232 tariff framework, base steel rates have risen; meanwhile a new rule lets multi-material industrial equipment qualify for a preferential 10% rate if at least 85% by weight of its steel is melted and poured in the US — a threshold loosened from the prior 95% to push supply chains toward “Made in America.” Compounded by geopolitically inflated freight and fuel surcharges, procurement managers are abandoning just-in-time (JIT) offshore sourcing in favor of higher domestic safety stock.在美国，钢铁制造业正迎来一轮庞大的资本支出热潮。在 232 条款关税框架下，钢材基础税率已升至较高水平；新政策同时规定，多材料制成的工业设备若其所含钢材中至少 85%（按重量计）为美国本土熔炼并浇铸，即可适用 10% 的优惠税率——门槛较此前的 95% 有所放宽，以激励供应链向“美国制造”倾斜。叠加地缘冲突推高的海运运费与燃油附加费，采购经理们正放弃原有的“准时制（JIT）”离岸采购模式，转向建立更高的本土安全库存。
03Global demand polarizes全球需求呈现两极分化›
Per worldsteel’s latest short-range outlook, 2026 global steel demand is seen growing just 0.3% (to 1.724 billion tonnes), regarded as a cyclical bottom, with a rebound to about +2.2% in 2027. Regionally the contrast is stark: China contracts (about -1.5% in 2026) amid an extended property and construction adjustment; India remains the fastest-growing major market, with demand up roughly 7% on infrastructure and autos; and the US, shielded by federal infrastructure spending and Buy-American rules, sees domestic consumption up about 1.7%.根据世界钢铁协会（worldsteel）最新短期需求预测，2026 年全球钢铁需求预计仅微幅增长 0.3%（至 17.24 亿吨），被视为周期触底，2027 年有望回升至约 +2.2%。地域上反差极大：中国受房地产及建筑市场持续调整影响，国内需求阶段性收缩（2026 年约 -1.5%）；印度继续蝉联全球增长最快的大型市场，基建与汽车工业推动需求增长约 7%；美国在联邦基建法案与“购买美国货”规则庇护下，国内消费预计增长约 1.7%。
04Decarbonization chemistry: blast furnace vs. direct-reduction routes脱碳化学：高炉与各类直接还原路线›
Traditional BF-BOF production burns large volumes of coke to reduce iron ore, releasing about 2.0–2.3 t CO₂ per tonne of crude steel. The industry is shifting to cleaner paths: scrap-based EAF (~0.3–0.7 t CO₂, clean power), natural-gas DRI-EAF (~1.1–1.4 t), and green-hydrogen DRI-EAF (~0.1–0.3 t). Scrap recycling consumes 5–7x less energy than primary smelting, making scrap a scarce strategic resource. The hydrogen route strips oxygen from ore via Fe₂O₃ + 3H₂ → 2Fe + 3H₂O; powered by renewables it can cut emissions to roughly 0.3 t CO₂ per tonne, with harmless water vapor as the only byproduct.传统高炉-转炉（BF-BOF）路线燃烧大量焦炭还原铁矿石，平均每吨粗钢释放约 2.0–2.3 吨 CO₂。行业正加速转向更清洁的路径：废钢循环电炉（EAF，清洁电力，约 0.3–0.7 吨）、天然气直接还原（NG-DRI-EAF，约 1.1–1.4 吨）、绿氢直接还原（H₂-DRI-EAF，约 0.1–0.3 吨）。废钢循环利用的能耗比原生冶炼低 5 到 7 倍，已被视为高度稀缺的战略资源。氢基路线以绿氢剥离铁矿石中的氧，反应式为 Fe₂O₃ + 3H₂ → 2Fe + 3H₂O；当由可再生能源供电时，碳排放可骤降至约 0.3 吨 CO₂/吨钢，唯一副产物是无害的水蒸气。
05NG-DRI: today’s most mature low-carbon bridge天然气直接还原：当前最成熟的低碳过渡路线›
With scrap supply limited and green hydrogen not yet cost-competitive at scale, natural-gas-based direct reduction is the most commercially mature low-carbon transition. Using hydrogen and carbon monoxide from gas reforming as reductants, it produces DRI in a shaft furnace for subsequent EAF steelmaking, cutting emissions about 50–60% versus the blast furnace. Modern shafts are designed “H₂-ready,” able to raise the hydrogen blend toward full-hydrogen operation as green supply arrives — a realistic bridge to zero carbon. Note: coal-based DRI can actually emit more than a blast furnace, so not all DRI routes are low-carbon.在废钢供应有限、绿氢成本与规模化尚未成熟的当下，天然气基直接还原已成为全球商业化最成熟的低碳过渡路线。该工艺以天然气重整产生的氢与一氧化碳为还原剂，在竖炉中将铁矿石还原为直接还原铁（DRI），再经电弧炉炼钢，碳排放较传统高炉降低约 50–60%。现代竖炉多设计为“氢就绪（H₂-ready）”，可随绿氢供应到位逐步提高掺氢比例直至全氢运行，被视为通向零碳的现实桥梁。需注意的是，以煤为还原剂的煤基 DRI 碳排放反而可能高于高炉——并非所有 DRI 路线都属低碳。
06Industrial breakthroughs: Stegra’s Boden plant and direct electrolysis工业级突破：Stegra 博登项目与直接电解›
Full-scale industrialization of hydrogen steel is now underway — while exposing capital-intensity limits. Sweden’s Stegra (formerly H2 Green Steel) Boden plant, the most-watched green-steel benchmark, pairs high-power EAFs with a 100% green-hydrogen direct-reduction shaft. In April 2026 a Wallenberg-led consortium provided about €1.4 billion of new financing toward completion, after cost overruns and delays pushed first steel from 2024 to around 2027. Separately, frontier players like Electra pursue low-temperature electrochemical-hydrometallurgical routes that bypass costly green-hydrogen production to electrolyze low-grade ore; its Colorado demo plant targets clean iron by mid-2026, though commercial scale-up has slipped toward 2029.氢能炼钢的全尺寸工业化已开始落地，但也暴露出资本强度与规模化的现实门槛。瑞典 Stegra（原 H2 Green Steel）的博登（Boden）项目是全球最受瞩目的绿色炼钢标杆，采用高功率电弧炉搭配 100% 绿氢的直接还原竖炉。2026 年 4 月，项目获瓦伦堡（Wallenberg）领衔财团约 14 亿欧元新融资，为完工开辟资金路径；此前因成本上升与多次延期，首炉钢投产已由最初的 2024 年推迟至 2027 年前后。另外，Electra 等前沿企业正推进低温、电化学—湿法冶金路线，绕过昂贵的绿氢制备阶段，直接电解低品位铁矿石；其科罗拉多示范厂计划于 2026 年中产出清洁铁，但商业化时间表已后推至 2029 年前后。
07Industry 4.0: digital dominance amid a labor gap工业 4.0：数字化主导与劳动力断层›
With heavy industry facing severe workforce aging and structural shortages (skilled welders, precision machinists), smart automation has shifted from frontier experiment to survival baseline. Modern rolling mills deploy industrial AI — LSTM networks and random forests reading real-time thermal-imaging and chemistry data — to predict metallurgical anomalies and surface defects during rolling and continuous casting, cutting scrap rates by nearly 40%. Meanwhile, surging demand from defense, aerospace and EVs for ultra-high-strength, corrosion-resistant, lightweight alloys is squeezing commodity crude and structural steel, pushing more production from outdoor work into highly controlled, robot-led modular smart factories.全球重工业普遍面临严重的劳动力老龄化与结构性短缺（如熟练焊接工与精密机械师的断层），智能自动化已从“前沿尝试”变为“生存底线”。现代轧钢厂深入普及工业人工智能——通过长短期记忆网络（LSTM）与随机森林等算法，依据实时热成像与化学数据，在滚轧与连铸阶段自动预测冶金异常与表面缺陷，使废品率降低近 40%。与此同时，国防、航空航天及新能源汽车（EV）对超高强度、耐腐蚀、轻量化特种合金的需求爆发式增长，正压缩粗钢及基础结构钢的生存空间，促使更多生产流程从室外作业转入高度可控、由机器人主导的模块化智能工厂。

Indonesia, AI and data centers印尼、AI 与数据中心

A summary of Indonesia’s AI and data-center developments as of June 2026 — power, capital and the resource constraints now shaping the build-out.截至 2026 年 6 月，印尼在 AI 与数据中心领域的最新动态——电力、资本，以及正在塑造这波建设的资源限制。

01Historic breakthrough — BDx secures 1.2 GW power commitment for AI expansion历史性突破：BDx 取得 1.2 GW 供电承诺以扩张 AI›
BDx Data Centers, a leading Asian data-center platform, announced landmark strategic agreements with Indonesia’s state utility PLN, securing a 1.2 GW power-capacity commitment for its Indonesian portfolio. The deal mainly powers its flagship CGK4 AI Campus in West Java (788 MVA of grid power) and high-voltage, AI-ready expansion in South Jakarta and Suryacipta. Officials stressed that stable, scalable power is the bedrock of Indonesia’s AI and digital-economy expansion.亚洲领先的数据中心平台 BDx Data Centers 宣布与印尼国营电力公司 PLN 达成里程碑式战略协议，为其印尼资产取得 1.2 GW 供电容量承诺。该协议主要供应位于西爪哇的旗舰 CGK4 AI 园区（788 MVA 电网电力），以及南雅加达与 Suryacipta 的高压、AI-ready 基础设施扩建。官方强调，稳定且可扩展的电力是印尼 AI 与数字经济扩张的根本基石。
02Chinese tech giant’s $5 billion AI project lands in Batam中国科技巨头 50 亿美元 AI 项目落脚巴淡岛›
Chinese data-center giant RangeIDC officially launched a US$5 billion (approx. IDR 88 trillion) mega AI data-center project in Batam’s Nongsa Digital Park (NDP). Indonesia’s Ministry of Transmigration responded by announcing a “Local High-Tech Talent Cultivation Initiative,” stressing the investment must benefit local youth through university collaborations so the next generation is prepared for core roles in global digital transformation.中国数据中心巨头 RangeIDC 正式启动位于巴淡岛 Nongsa Digital Park（NDP）、总额 50 亿美元（约 88 万亿印尼盾）的大型 AI 数据中心项目。印尼移民部随即宣布推出「在地高科技人才培育计划」，强调这笔投资必须透过大学合作直接惠及本地青年，确保新一代具备投入全球数字转型核心职位的能力。
03Sovereign wealth funds (INA & Danantara) aggressively back AI compute主权基金（INA 与 Danantara）大力押注 AI 算力›
Responding to the global $3 trillion wave into data centers, Indonesia’s sovereign wealth fund INA will allocate up to 30% of its annual capital to digital infrastructure; its portfolio data-center capacity (74 MW) is already 100% pre-leased. INA’s CIO emphasised a “non-aligned” stance amid US–China tech rivalry to welcome global capital, while the broader-mandate fund Danantara is actively seeking to anchor major AI data-center projects — signalling unprecedented state-backed financial support.因应全球涌入数据中心的 3 万亿美元投资潮，印尼主权基金 INA（印尼投资管理局）宣布将年度资本至多 30% 专门配置于数字基础设施；其投资组合中的数据中心容量（74 MW）已达 100% 预租率。INA 投资长强调，在美中科技角力下印尼维持「不结盟」立场以广纳全球资本；另一授权更广的国家基金 Danantara 也积极寻求锚定大型 AI 数据中心项目，显示前所未有的国家级资金支持。
04Forward blueprint — drafting the “Eastern Indonesia expansion blueprint”前瞻蓝图：起草「印尼东部扩展蓝图」›
With the vast majority of Indonesia’s 170+ data centers concentrated in western hubs like Greater Jakarta and Batam, the Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs (Komdigi) is drafting a “Sovereign Blueprint for Private Data Center Expansion in Eastern Indonesia,” aiming to steer future AI data centers toward distributed eastern layouts that tap local alternative and green energy for nationwide data sovereignty and infrastructure balance.印尼 170 多座数据中心绝大多数集中于大雅加达与巴淡岛等西部枢纽，通讯与数字事务部（Komdigi）正起草「印尼东部民营数据中心扩展主权蓝图」，目标引导未来 AI 数据中心朝东部分散式布局发展，善用当地替代与绿色能源，以确保全国数据主权与基础设施平衡。
05Industry concern — the AI boom sparks a “water crisis”产业隐忧：AI 热潮引发「水资源危机」›
As high-density AI servers (such as NVIDIA’s latest GB200 systems) flood in, demand for water- and liquid-cooling has surged. Recent environmental reports warn that in Batam alone, 9 planned and existing AI data centers could consume 29 million liters of water per day (equivalent to 30,000 residents), nearly 8% of the island’s total supply. Balancing this “tech gold rush” with local water sustainability has become the government’s most pressing second-half challenge.随着高密度 AI 服务器（如 NVIDIA 最新的 GB200 系统）大量涌入，水冷与液冷系统需求激增。近期环境报告警告，仅巴淡岛一地，9 座规划中与既有的 AI 数据中心预计每日耗水 2,900 万公升（相当于 3 万名居民用量），吞掉全岛近 8% 供水。如何在这场「科技淘金热」与当地水资源可持续之间取得平衡，已成为印尼政府下半年最迫切的课题。
Early June 20262026 年 6 月初 Indonesia · Politics · Macro · Diplomacy印尼 · 内政 · 财经 · 外交

Indonesia briefing — domestic affairs, FX markets and regional diplomacy印尼要闻 — 内政、汇市与区域外交

Key Indonesian developments as of early June 2026 — several indicative shifts across domestic policy, currency markets and regional diplomacy this week.截至 2026 年 6 月初的印尼核心动态——本周在内政、财经汇市与区域外交上出现数项指标性变动。

01Flagship “free nutritious lunch” agency chief replaced旗舰政策「免费营养午餐」机构首长遭撤换›
On 2 June, President Prabowo Subianto removed National Nutrition Agency (BGN) head Dadan Hindayana, installing his deputy. The campaign’s flagship programme has been troubled since launching last January, causing mass food poisoning among tens of thousands of students and drawing anti-corruption scrutiny over budget irregularities; the former chief had recently floated expanding it “to Saudi Arabia.” Prabowo cited “food safety quality and management problems” for the leadership overhaul.总统普拉博沃于 6 月 2 日免去国家营养局（BGN）局长 Dadan Hindayana 职务，改由其副手接任。这项竞选核心旗舰政策自去年 1 月上路后风波不断，已累计导致数万名学生集体食物中毒，并遭廉政组织调查预算违规；前局长近日更抛出将计划「扩大至沙特阿拉伯」的提议。普拉博沃以「食品安全质量与管理问题」为由进行领导层大洗牌。
02Pancasila Day address — Prabowo declares war on “corruption and smuggling networks”建国五原则纪念日演说，普拉博沃向「贪腐与走私集团」宣战›
On 1 June’s Pancasila Day, Prabowo delivered a forceful address, reiterating that Indonesia must drive a thorough “economic transformation” and warning that pushing single-window state-controlled commodity exports and industrial downstreaming will meet resistance from vested interests: “We will confront head-on those groups indulging in corruption, smuggling and the illegal economy.” The message signalled to global investors his resolve to curb resource outflows and enforce economic fairness.6 月 1 日建国五原则（Pancasila）纪念日，普拉博沃发表强硬演说，重申印尼必须彻底推动「经济转型」，并警告政府在推动大宗商品「单一窗口国营专营出口」及产业下游化时，必将遭既得利益者抵抗：「我们将正面迎战那些耽溺于贪腐、走私与非法经济活动的群体。」藉此向全球投资人展现整顿资源外流、落实经济公平的决心。
03Rupiah tests 17,900 as the central bank imposes FX purchase limits印尼盾面临 17,900 保卫战，央行祭出外汇限购铁腕›
Hit by a prolonged Middle East situation, high international energy prices and a strengthening dollar, the rupiah weakened sharply, briefly breaching 17,900 per US dollar. Bank Indonesia (BI) and the finance ministry jointly imposed strict FX purchase limits from 2 June: conversions lacking proof of underlying assets (such as import invoices or debt-repayment evidence) are capped at US$25,000 per person per transaction per month, targeting speculative trading.受中东局势延宕、国际能源价格高企及美元走强冲击，印尼盾近期大幅走弱，一度跌破 1 美元兑 17,900 印尼盾关卡。央行（BI）与财政部紧急联手，自 6 月 2 日起实施严格外汇购买限额：凡缺乏实质资产证明（如进口发票或债务偿还证明）者，每人每月每笔兑换上限压低至 25,000 美元，全力打击投机炒汇。
04Indonesia and Malaysia convene bilateral commission (JCBC)印尼与马来西亚召开双边高层会议（JCBC）›
On 4 June, Malaysia’s foreign minister led a senior delegation to Jakarta to co-chair the 17th Malaysia–Indonesia Joint Commission for Bilateral Cooperation (JCBC) with Indonesian counterpart Sugiono. Against continued global supply-chain volatility, the two prioritised economic resilience, energy security and food supply, while advancing longer-term agendas including border trade, maritime boundary delimitation and halal-industry cooperation.马来西亚外长于 6 月 4 日率高阶代表团抵达雅加达，与印尼外长 Sugiono 共同主持第 17 届马印双边合作联合委员会（JCBC）。在全球供应链持续动荡下，两国将协商重点放在经济抗险、能源安全与粮食供应，并就边境贸易、海上边界划定及清真产业合作等长期议题展开具体对接。
